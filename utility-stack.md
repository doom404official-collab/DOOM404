# DOOM404 Utility Stack

The DOOM404 Litepaper defines five progressive applications. Market-cap levels are **development gates**, not automatic launch triggers.

## $10M — DOOM AI — Planned
Ecosystem intelligence layer: a DOOM404-specific AI agent intended to know the ecosystem, understand community discussions and act as an interface to future applications.

Planned functions include answering DOOM404 questions, summarising proposals/updates/discussions, helping users navigate the ecosystem, identifying emerging GLITCHES in community activity, and becoming the interface for future DOOM404 tools.

## $25M — GLITCH Scanner — Planned
On-chain and project diagnostics. Proposed areas include token and wallet analysis, holder distribution and concentration, unusual on-chain behaviour, liquidity conditions, project transparency indicators and community-reported concerns.

## $50M — Community Intelligence — Planned
Collective knowledge: turning community observations, research and discussions into structured and searchable intelligence. The Litepaper notes that not all submissions are verified facts and calls for safeguards against manipulation.

## $100M — Reputation & Contributor Network — Planned
Identity and contribution infrastructure based on useful participation rather than token ownership alone. Concepts include DOOM ID, DOOM REP, missions, contributor roles/recognition and potential future use in community governance.

## $250M — Business404 — Exploratory
Exploration of whether blockchain-based technology can support compliant digital representations of defined business interests, rights or assets in small, unlisted businesses.

Business404 is explicitly exploratory. No tokenised securities, business ownership or investment products are offered at this stage. Implementation would require significant legal, regulatory, technical and commercial development.

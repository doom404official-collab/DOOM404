# DOOM404 Genesis Drop 001 — Phase 1 market policy

Decision: Indian customer shipping/orders are on hold for Phase 1. Focus on international markets.

Priority candidate destinations: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, EU countries, Australia, Japan, Singapore, United Arab Emirates.

All markets are *candidates*, not yet approved for checkout. Before activation verify per-SKU Printful delivery eligibility, actual rates, taxes/duties, return policies, merchant payment gateway support and end-to-end test orders.

Shopify checkout must exclude India as a shipping destination during Phase 1. Do not merely hide India in frontend UI; configure and test Shopify shipping zones/markets and fulfillment restrictions server-side. Do not activate checkout until user approval and integration validation.

No customer purchases, production orders, or live website changes are authorized by this policy document.

Phase 2: revisit India with suitable local supplier or verified import eligibility and shipping economics.

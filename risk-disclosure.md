# Funding, Risks & Responsibility

## Potential Development Funding Sources

The Litepaper identifies a multi-source model:

- Development Treasury — 2% allocation for ecosystem development, subject to governance and transparency.
- Platform / Creator Revenue — potential allocation from platform mechanisms.
- Utility Revenue — future application-specific revenue models.
- Grants & Partnerships — ecosystem grants and strategic collaborations.
- Community Funding — community-approved, voluntary initiatives.
- External Capital — potential future investment for specific applications.

**No development funding source is guaranteed.**

## Key Risks

- **Market Risk** — High volatility and potential loss of value.
- **Regulatory Risk** — Changing regulations and legal uncertainty.
- **Technical Risk** — Development delays or technical challenges.
- **Execution Risk** — Utilities may be delayed, changed or cancelled.
- **Treasury / Key Management Risk** — Security of project-controlled assets.
- **Adoption Risk** — Utilities may not achieve expected usage.

Refer to the official Litepaper v1.0 for the complete project presentation.

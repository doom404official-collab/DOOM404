## Evidence sheet per mint
Record mint/program, chain/slot/timestamp, provider, transaction signatures, pool identifiers, confidence, completeness and snapshot freshness.
- Token Security: parsed mint/freeze authorities, Token-2022 effective fee epoch, maximum fee, config authority, extension applicability.
- Holder Intelligence: top-20 owner-resolved balances and mint supply; verified transfer pairs, direct/repeated/indirect counts, excluded infrastructure, history exhaustion, creator/team exposure and independent wallets.
- Creator Intelligence: launch origin attribution; attributable holdings; verified swaps vs transfers; 1h/24h/7d sell-through; comparable historical launches and coverage.
- Liquidity Quality & Safety: verified pool reserves, independently classified LP controls, market-cap denominator, executable sell quote, independent pool concentration, 30-day reserve changes.

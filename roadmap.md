# DOOM404 Roadmap

The roadmap is aligned with market-cap development gates and ecosystem readiness.

| Stage | Focus | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 0 | Community | Launch token, establish channels and grow the community |
| $10M | DOOM AI | Build the ecosystem intelligence layer |
| $25M | GLITCH Scanner | Launch structured diagnostics |
| $50M | Community Intelligence | Turn community knowledge into intelligence |
| $100M | Reputation Network | Build identity and contribution infrastructure |
| $250M | Business404 | Evaluate real-world applications after extensive feasibility work |

These are development gates, not promises that an application will automatically launch when a particular market capitalization is reached.
